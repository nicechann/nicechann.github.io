MellowSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Overview

MellowSnap is a camera and photo editing app provided by Nicechann Studio. The app allows users to capture photos and videos, import photos from their library, edit them with filters and adjustments, and save the results to their device.

Data Collection

MellowSnap does not require users to create an account and does not collect personal information for user identification.

Photos and videos selected or captured by the user are processed on the device for editing and saving. MellowSnap does not upload the user’s photos or videos to Nicechann Studio servers.

Photos and Videos

MellowSnap accesses photos and videos only when the user grants permission or selects media to edit. Edited results are saved to the user’s device or photo library based on the user’s action.

The app does not use the user’s photos or videos for advertising, tracking, or server-side analysis.

Permissions

  • Camera: Used to capture photos and videos.
  • Microphone: Used only when recording videos with audio.
  • Photo Library: Used to import photos and videos and to save edited results.

In-App Purchases

MellowSnap Pro is offered through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Payment processing is handled by Apple. MellowSnap does not access or store the user’s payment card information.

Third-Party Sharing

MellowSnap does not sell user data. The app does not upload photos, videos, or personal data to Nicechann Studio servers.

Apple platform services, including App Store purchases, are handled according to Apple’s own systems and policies.

Children’s Privacy

MellowSnap is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require account registration or personal profile information.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app changes or when legal, technical, or operational requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
