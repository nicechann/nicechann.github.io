Frequently Asked Questions

  • If photos are not saved, please check Photo Library permission in iOS Settings.
  • If the camera preview is not available, please check Camera permission in iOS Settings.
  • If video recording does not include sound, please check Microphone permission in iOS Settings.
  • If Pro features are not unlocked after purchase, please try “Restore Purchases” in the app.
  • If product information is not displayed, please check your network connection and App Store account status.

About MellowSnap

MellowSnap is a preset camera and photo editing app for capturing and editing everyday moments with soft, natural filters.

You can choose a preset before shooting, import photos from your library, compare before and after, and apply the same filter to multiple photos at once.

MellowSnap Pro

MellowSnap Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks Pro filters and batch editing features. If you have already purchased Pro, use “Restore Purchases” in the app with the same Apple ID.
