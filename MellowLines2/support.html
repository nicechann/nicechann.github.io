FAQ

How do I play?

Start at the blue start cell and draw one continuous line to the goal. Every playable cell must be visited exactly once. If you take a wrong route, retrace the line to backtrack.

Why do some levels have a timer?

The opening levels ease you into the rule. Later levels add a time limit, and Challenge levels are designed to provide a stronger timed test.

How do stars work?

From Level 11 onward, a first clear within the time limit can earn ★1. A first successful Challenge clear can earn ★5. Replaying a completed level does not repeatedly grant the same first-clear reward.

What can I use stars for?

Stars can be exchanged in the Store. A Hint costs ★5 and an Answer Fill costs ★10.

How do hints work?

A hint shows upcoming cells from your current route. Repeated hint use can reveal more of the route. If your current path is blocked, the hint system may indicate where to backtrack.

What is Answer Fill?

Answer Fill completes the current puzzle solution for you. It is intended as an optional assist when you want to continue progressing.

What does Pro do?

Pro is a non-consumable in-app purchase that removes automatically displayed banner and interstitial ads. Optional rewarded-ad choices may still be available when you choose to use them.

How do I restore Pro?

Open the Store or Settings in MellowLines 2 and choose Restore Purchases. Purchases are verified through Apple StoreKit.

Can consumable Hint or Answer Fill packs be restored?

Consumable item packs are handled differently from the non-consumable Pro purchase and are not restored through the standard Restore Purchases action after they have been consumed.

Game Center is not updating. What should I check?

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings. Apple may sometimes take a short time to display updated leaderboard results.
