Privacy

Your notes stay yours.

Cards and memos are stored on your device and, when you enable iCloud sync, in your Apple iCloud account. PinDeck does not run a separate account system for your note content. The free iOS version may show ads; the Mac app does not include advertising or tracking SDKs.
