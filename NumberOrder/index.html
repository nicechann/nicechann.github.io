NumberOrder

A fast number-order puzzle game where you read the rule and tap numbers in the correct order.

View Features Support Privacy Policy

Key Features

  • Read the rule and tap the numbers in the correct order.
  • Play Sprint mode for fast, focused number-order challenges.
  • Try Daily Challenge and update your best record every day.
  • Practice without a time limit to learn each rule at your own pace.
  • Customize your profile, country, rules, and visual themes.
  • Compete through Game Center leaderboards.

Game Modes

  • Sprint: Tap quickly before time runs out.
  • Daily: Challenge a new daily rule and record.
  • Practice: Learn the rules with no time limit.
