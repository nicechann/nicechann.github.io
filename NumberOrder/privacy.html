Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NumberOrder handles information when you use the app. NumberOrder is developed by NICECHANN STUDIO.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at nicechann@gmail.com.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

NumberOrder may store gameplay records, best scores, settings, selected themes, onboarding status, notification preferences, and purchase status on your device. This information is used to provide app functionality and improve your gameplay experience.

2. Game Center

NumberOrder may use Apple Game Center for leaderboards and score submission. If you use Game Center, your score and leaderboard information may be processed by Apple according to Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

3. In-App Purchases

NumberOrder may offer an in-app purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment card information.

4. Advertising

NumberOrder may display ads using Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising data, app interactions, crash logs, and performance data for purposes such as ad delivery, fraud prevention, analytics, and improving service performance.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, please visit: Google Partner Sites Policy.

5. Consent Management

NumberOrder may use Google User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent where required by applicable laws and regulations. You may be shown privacy or consent options depending on your region and app settings.

6. Notifications

NumberOrder may request permission to send local notifications, such as Daily Challenge reminders. These notifications are managed on your device. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Some data may be processed by third-party services used by the app, such as Apple services for purchases and Game Center, and Google services for advertising and consent management.

8. Children

NumberOrder is not specifically directed to children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
