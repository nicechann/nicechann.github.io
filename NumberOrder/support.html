FAQ

How do I play?

Read the rule shown on each wave, then tap the numbers in the correct order as quickly as possible.

What is Sprint mode?

Sprint is a fast challenge mode where you follow changing rules and try to earn a high score before time runs out.

What is Daily Challenge?

Daily Challenge gives you a daily rule and record challenge. You can try to improve your score every day.

How does Practice mode work?

Practice mode has no time limit, so you can learn the rules and improve your accuracy at your own pace.

I purchased Remove Ads. How can I restore it?

Open the app settings and use Restore Purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

Game Center leaderboard is not updating.

Please make sure you are signed in to Game Center on your device. Some leaderboard updates may take time to appear.
