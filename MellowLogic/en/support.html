Frequently asked questions

How do I start?

Tap Start Investigation on the home screen. You can reopen How to Play and the practice puzzle in Settings.

Where can characters stand?

There must be exactly one person in each row and column. Characters may stand on chairs and rugs, but cannot stand on darkened cells containing tables, plants, or display cabinets. Check the object rules above the clue list for each puzzle.

What does right next to mean?

It means a cell directly above, below, left, or right. Diagonals do not count. Adjacent cells still count as next to each other across an area boundary.

What if I get stuck?

Tap clues to highlight relevant locations, and use cell or character exclusion marks to rule out possibilities. You can also use the lightbulb hint, undo, and redo.

How many hints do I get?

You start with 5 hints, and opening a new hint uses 1. Viewing the same hint again or opening more of its explanation is free, and hints in the tutorial cases are free too. When they run out, you can choose to watch an ad for 2 more, or get Season Pro for unlimited hints in that season. You can always keep solving from the clues without hints.

Are there ads?

Only when you've run out of hints and tap “Watch an ad for 2 hints” yourself. There are no banner or forced ads. Hints are added only if you watch the ad to the end. Watching an ad needs an internet connection.

What is Season Pro?

A one-time purchase for each season that gives unlimited hints in every case of that season. It doesn't apply to other seasons. After reinstalling or on a new device, bring it back with Restore Purchases in Settings. Restoring brings back Pro only; progress and your hint balance stay on the device. To request a refund, contact Apple.

Must I place everyone to finish?

If you know the culprit, submit your answer using the culprit button. You can also solve the case by placing every character so that all clues are satisfied.

How do I delete my progress?

Reset Progress in Settings deletes puzzle progress and completion records. Playtest logs are separate; use Clear Logs to delete them too. These actions cannot be undone.

Are play logs sent automatically?

No. Logs stay on your device. They leave the app only when you export them and choose a sharing destination. Clearing logs in the app does not remove copies already shared.

Can I play in English?

MellowLogic supports Korean and English. Check the per-app language setting for MellowLogic in iOS Settings.
