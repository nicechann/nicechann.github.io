Privacy Policy

MellowLogic · Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Applies to version 1.0.0 and later

In MellowLogic (the "app"), an iOS app made by Nicechann Studio ("we"), we don't collect personal data. Rewarded ads, which appear only when you choose to watch one, are provided by Google, and Google may process the information in section 2 while showing an ad.

1. What we don't collect

  • The app doesn't ask for an account, sign-in, name or contact details. We don't run servers, and nothing is sent to us.
  • The app contains no analytics or tracking tools and doesn't ask for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission, so neither the app nor the ad SDK receives the advertising identifier (IDFA).

2. Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

  • An ad is loaded and shown only when you run out of hints and tap Watch an ad for 2 hints. There are no banner ads and no forced full-screen ads.
  • Watching an ad needs an internet connection. Puzzles can be played offline.
  • When loading and showing an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may send the following to Google for processing (based on Google's public disclosure):
    • A device identifier (an app- or developer-scoped identifier, not the advertising identifier, because ATT isn't granted)
    • Approximate location estimated from your IP address
    • The ads you saw and your interactions with them (impressions, taps, video views and so on)
    • App launch and performance information and diagnostic information about errors
  • Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and for analytics, as described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
  • In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, Google's consent form appears the first time you watch an ad. You can change your choices any time in Settings › Ad privacy settings.

3. In-app purchases (Season Pro)

  • Season Pro payments are handled by the Apple App Store. We never receive card numbers or other payment details.
  • To check what you've bought, the app reads Apple's transaction information (StoreKit) on your device and stores the last confirmed purchase on the device. Restoring purchases uses the transactions in your Apple Account.

4. What stays on your device

To work, the app stores the following only on your device. We can't see it.

  • Puzzle progress: Each puzzle's board (placements, X marks, clue checks), elapsed time, hints used, completions, and the list of puzzles that have been unlocked (so an update that adds puzzles doesn't lock them again) (The app's private storage)
  • Solution replays: The order of board changes (placements, X marks, clue checks, applied hints, undos and so on, up to 2,000 moves per attempt) for the current attempt and the last solved attempt. Used by the Replay solution screen and never included in exports (The app's private storage (same folder as progress))
  • Play records: For each attempt where you actually played: time taken, moves, undos, resets, wrong finishes, hints used and when it was solved. Attempts you only opened are not saved, and only the latest 1,000 are kept (The app's private storage (separate from progress))
  • Hint balance: Free hints left, hints already charged, and the IDs of ad rewards already credited (so the same reward is never given twice) (The app's private storage (separate from progress))
  • Unapplied ad rewards: If an ad reward was earned but could not yet be added to the balance, its reward ID and season (deleted as soon as it is added) (App settings (UserDefaults))
  • Season Pro status: The product names of the last confirmed purchases (App settings (UserDefaults))
  • Display settings: Whether the how-to-play guide was shown, which clue types have already been explained, and the haptics setting (App settings (UserDefaults))

If device backup (iCloud or a computer) is turned on, this data may be included in backups according to Apple's backup settings.

5. Exporting play records

Only when you tap Export records in Settings does the app create a JSON file of your play records and open the iOS share sheet.

  • The file contains the play records above plus the app version, iOS version, device model name (for example, iPhone15,2), app language, text size setting, whether VoiceOver is on, and the puzzle pack name. It contains no name, contact details or device identifiers.
  • The file goes only where you choose in the share sheet (Mail, Messages, Files and so on). The app never sends it by itself.
  • The temporary file the app created is deleted when the share sheet closes. Copies you've already sent to other apps or people can't be deleted by the app and are subject to the recipient's policies.

6. How to delete data

  • Settings › Playtest records › Delete records: deletes the play records on this device and any temporary export file the app created. Puzzle progress and completions stay.
  • Settings › Reset progress: deletes progress, completions and solution replays for every puzzle, the list of unlocked puzzles and the list of explained clue types. Play records and your hint balance stay.
  • Deleting the app: removes all app data stored on the device, including the hint balance (copies in device backups follow your backup settings). Season Pro can be brought back after reinstalling with Restore Purchases.
  • Information Google processed for ads is governed by Google's policies and tools (links in section 2).

Deleted data can't be recovered.

7. Information in support requests

If you email us, we use your email address, message and any screenshots or records you choose to attach only to answer and troubleshoot. Please don't send sensitive information. Support materials are kept only as long as needed to handle your request, and you can ask us to delete them at the same email address. Email service providers may process messages.

8. This website

This informational website has no analytics or advertising tracking code. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process access information such as IP addresses to provide and secure the service (GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Apple services such as device backups and diagnostics are governed by the Apple Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

9. Children

The app doesn't ask for age or contact details, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one and follow section 2. If you believe a support message contains a child's personal data, ask us to delete it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version and its effective date on this page. If a change would mean processing more information, we'll announce it before the app update.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or how information is handled, email nicechann@gmail.com or visit the support page.
