Privacy Policy

MellowLogic · Effective November 6, 2026

Your investigation stays on your device. Sharing play logs is your choice.

1. Operator and scope

Nicechann Studio provides the MellowLogic app and this informational website. For privacy questions, contact nicechann@gmail.com.

2. App collection and tracking

MellowLogic does not require an account and does not automatically send personal information or play logs to developer-operated servers. The app does not include advertising or user-tracking SDKs.

3. Information stored on your device

The app stores puzzle progress, character placements and exclusion marks, completion records, playtest logs, tutorial status, and haptic preferences on your device. Play logs may include puzzle and attempt identifiers, play dates and active time, counts of actions, undos, resets and incorrect answers, hint usage, and completion status. This supports gameplay and reviewing the play experience; it is not automatically uploaded.

4. Optional log exports

If you choose Export Playtest Logs in Settings and select a destination in the iOS share sheet, the file is shared with that service or recipient. In addition to play logs, exports may include app and content versions, OS version, device model code, language, text size, VoiceOver status, and export time. They do not include your device name or advertising identifier. The destination handles the file under its own policies. Temporary export files in the app are cleaned up when sharing closes or logs are cleared, but externally saved copies may remain.

5. Retention and deletion

Reset Progress in Settings deletes puzzle progress and completion records while keeping playtest logs. Use Clear Logs separately to delete the playtest logs stored on your device. Deleting the app generally removes its local data. Manage device backups and previously exported files separately through iOS or the relevant storage service.

6. Support communications

If you email us, we process your email address, message, and any screenshots or logs you voluntarily attach to respond and troubleshoot. Do not send sensitive information. Support materials are retained only as needed to handle your request; you can request deletion at the same email address. Email service providers may process messages.

7. Website and external services

This website has no added analytics or advertising tracking code. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process access information such as IP addresses to provide and secure the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Apple services, including iOS backups and diagnostics, are governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

8. Children’s privacy

The app does not ask for age or contact details and does not knowingly collect personal information from users, including children. Contact us to request deletion if you believe a support message contains a child’s personal information.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this page if features or data practices change. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email nicechann@gmail.com.
