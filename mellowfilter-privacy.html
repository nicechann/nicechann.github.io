Privacy Policy

MellowFilter is an editing app that imports photos and videos from the user's photo library and applies filters on the device.

Last updated: 2026-05-14

1. Information we collect

MellowFilter does not require account registration or login. The app does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, precise location, payment information, or other personal information on its own server.

2. Photo library access

MellowFilter may request access to the user's photo library to import photos and videos selected by the user and to save edited results back to the photo library.

  • Importing photos and videos: used to edit the media selected by the user.
  • Saving edited results: used to store edited photos or videos in the user's photo library.

Photos and videos are processed on the user's device. MellowFilter does not upload the selected media to its own server.

3. Recipes and settings

Saved recipes, favorite filters, recent filter history, and app settings may be stored locally on the user's device to provide app functionality.

4. Sharing with third parties

MellowFilter does not sell or share personal information with third parties.
