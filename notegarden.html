🌿NoteGarden

A quiet place for your day.

NoteGarden is a calm daily journal built around one entry a day. Write a few lines, add today’s photos, choose how you felt, and let it save itself.

About NoteGarden

NoteGarden keeps journaling simple: one page per day. A gentle daily prompt helps you start, photos bring the day back, and a mood marks how it felt. Everything saves automatically, and you can look back anytime through a calendar, a list, or a photo timeline.

Key Features

  • One entry a day, with a gentle daily writing prompt
  • Attach photos and revisit them in a photo timeline
  • Track your mood and see it across calendar and list views
  • “On This Day” — revisit entries from the same date in past years
  • iCloud sync keeps your entries and photos on your own devices
  • File backup & restore — export everything to a single file, import anytime
  • App Lock with Face ID / Touch ID
  • 30-day Trash with restore, and automatic saving
  • Available in English, 日本語, and 한국어

Yours, and only yours

NoteGarden is a paid app with no ads and no tracking. Your writing and photos stay on your device and in your own private iCloud — never on our servers. It’s a one-time purchase through Apple’s App Store.
